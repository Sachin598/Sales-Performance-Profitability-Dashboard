 📊 Sales Performance & Profitability Dashboard

Project Overview
An interactive 3-page Power BI dashboard designed to analyze business sales performance, profitability, product performance, customer behavior, and regional sales trends.

🎯 Project Objectives
- Analyze overall sales and profit performance.
- Identify top-performing products.
- Evaluate product profitability.
- Understand customer purchasing behavior.
- Analyze regional sales distribution.
- Monitor key business KPIs.

 📌 Dashboard Pages

 1. Business Performance Overview
- Total Sales
- Total Profit
- Total Orders
- Total Customers
- Total Quantity
- Profit Margin
- Monthly Sales Trend
- Profit by Product Category
- Sales by Region

 2. Product & Profitability Analysis
- Top 10 Products by Sales
- Sales vs Profit by Product
- Average Discount vs Total Profit
- Profit by Sub-Category

3. Customer & Regional Analysis
- Total Customers
- Total Sales
- Total Orders
- Average Sales per Customer
- Sales Performance by City & Region (Map)
- Top 10 Customers by Sales
- Sales by Customer Segment

 🛠️ Tools & Technologies
- Microsoft Power BI
- Power Query
- DAX
- Data Visualization
- Business Intelligence

 📈 Key Business Insights
- Technology is the highest profit-generating category.
- West region contributes the highest sales.
- Product-level analysis identifies top revenue-generating products.
- Customer segmentation helps understand purchasing patterns.
